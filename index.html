Na-ion electrolyte process flows

Electrolyte-level process flows for the NaPF₆ liquid baseline and four solid or gel alternatives. Each card is one unit process. Every card lists the inputs you will need as LCI flows and TEA line items, and tags the likely hotspots. The system boundary stops at the cell. Module, pack and BMS are held identical across scenarios and dropped.
