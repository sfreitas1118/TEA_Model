# TEA_Model

Techno-economic analysis (TEA) for ENGG 192: LCA/TEA of sodium-ion battery electrolytes (solid-state and gel polymer).

Live page: https://sfreitas1118.github.io/TEA_Model/

## Contents

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Process flows for the NaPF₆ liquid baseline and oxide (NASICON), sulfide, halide and PEO–PPC–NASICON composite electrolytes, with LCA/TEA hotspots, a worst-case H₂S bound, a per-kWh electrolyte cost calculator, and the baseline TEA results (cell cost, material prices, sensitivity, benchmarks, conclusions). |
| `SF_TEA_tables.xlsx` | Stationary-storage Na-ion cell plant TEA (8 GWh/yr, 1 kWh functional unit), based on J. Chitu's Na-ion EV model: inputs, mass balance, CapEx, OpEx, levelized cell cost and sensitivity, with Argonne reference tables. |

## Baseline result

- Levelized cell cost: **$52.63/kWh**.
- Prussian White, hard carbon and sodium-ion electrolyte prices: Shanghai Metals Market (28 Aug 2026). Other materials: Argonne BatPaC 2024.

In the spreadsheet, blue cells are inputs and black cells are formulas.
